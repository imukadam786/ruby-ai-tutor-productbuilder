## Special case — ruler questions (cropping does NOT fix these)

These ask the student to physically measure the printed page. On a phone or laptop the on-screen size will not match the printed exam, so any measurement they take would be wrong.

| Paper | Sub-Q | Task |
|---|---|---|
| P2 2021 | 2.7.2 | Measure inner length of bedroom 2 |
| P2 2023 | 2.1.5(a) | Measure a length on the plan |
| P2 2024 | 2.1.6(b) | Measure the plan to find the scale |
| P2 2025 | 2.2.4 | Measure the 500 m bar scale |

**Current handling:** the measured value is stated in the question, so the student can still do the scale calculation (the actual maths) — only the measuring step is given. Recommend leaving as-is, or rewording to "the plan measures X cm — now calculate…".
