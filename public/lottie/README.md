# Ruby celebration Lottie assets

Drop the 5 Lottie **JSON** files here with these exact names. The app loads them
by path (`/lottie/<name>.json`) at runtime. Download each from its LottieFiles
page via **Download → Lottie JSON** (not GIF/MP4).

| Filename            | Source                                                                 | Recolour                |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| `ruby-gem.json`     | Diamond — https://lottiefiles.com/8214-diamond                          | → #BE1832 (done in repo)|
| `success-burst.json`| Success Burst — https://lottiefiles.com/53083-success-burst             | → #BE1832 (done in repo)|
| `confetti.json`     | Confetti effects — https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/confetti-effects-lottie-animation-2naH1Qyz3l | keep multicolour |
| `trophy.json`       | Trophy — https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/trophy-yEGPe40FVr       | keep                    |
| `streak-fire.json`  | Fire — https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/fire-animation-zZ0tyv7uSp | keep flame colours      |

Recolouring of `ruby-gem` and `success-burst` to the brand ruby (#BE1832) is
applied in-repo after the originals are placed — you do not need to recolour.
