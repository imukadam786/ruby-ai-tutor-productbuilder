## L13.T1.A2 — Le Chatelier & industrial processes

**L13.T1.A2.q01** · ERR_LECHATELIER_STATEMENT  
Q: State LE CHATELIER'S PRINCIPLE.  
- ✗ A system at equilibrium always shifts to make more product.  
- ✗ Disturbing an equilibrium stops the reaction entirely.  
- ✅ When an equilibrium is disturbed, the system shifts to oppose the change and re-establish equilibrium.  

**L13.T1.A2.q02** · ERR_LECHATELIER_CONCENTRATION  
Q: The equilibrium 2CrO₄²⁻ (yellow) + 2H⁺ ⇌ Cr₂O₇²⁻ (orange) + H₂O is observed. Adding ACID to the solution causes the colour to change from yellow to orange. Explain.  
- ✅ Adding H⁺ shifts the equilibrium right (to use up H⁺), forming more orange Cr₂O₇²⁻.  
- ✗ Adding acid shifts it left, making more yellow CrO₄²⁻.  
- ✗ Adding acid has no effect on this equilibrium.  

**L13.T1.A2.q04** · ERR_LECHATELIER_TEMPERATURE, ERR_KC_TEMPERATURE  
Q: How does INCREASING the temperature affect an EXOTHERMIC equilibrium reaction?  
- ✗ It shifts forward — heat speeds up product formation.  
- ✅ It shifts in the endothermic (reverse) direction, absorbing the added heat.  
- ✗ There is no shift — temperature doesn't move equilibrium.  

**L13.T1.A2.q05** · ERR_LECHATELIER_TEMPERATURE, ERR_EXOTHERMIC_DIRECTION  
Q: For the exothermic reaction H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI (ΔH < 0). On a concentration-time graph, [HI] suddenly DECREASES at t₂. Was the system HEATED or COOLED?  
- ✗ Cooled — lower temperature favours the reverse reaction.  
- ✗ Neither — the [HI] change is unrelated to temperature.  
- ✅ Heated — for an exothermic forward reaction, higher T favours the reverse, consuming HI.  

**L13.T1.A2.q06** · ERR_LECHATELIER_TEMPERATURE, ERR_KC_TEMPERATURE  
Q: For 2HI(g) ⇌ H₂(g) + I₂(g): Kc at 721 K = 0.02 and at 850 K = 0.09. Is the forward reaction EXOTHERMIC or ENDOTHERMIC?  
- ✅ Endothermic — Kc rises with temperature, so higher T favours the forward (endothermic) direction.  
- ✗ Exothermic — a rising Kc means more heat is released.  
- ✗ Neither — Kc does not depend on temperature.  

**L13.T1.A2.q08** · ERR_LECHATELIER_PRESSURE, ERR_GAS_MOLES_COUNT  
Q: How does INCREASING the PRESSURE affect the equilibrium N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g)?  
- ✗ It shifts left, toward the 4 moles of reactant gas.  
- ✅ It shifts right (to the products) — the side with fewer moles of gas (2 vs 4).  
- ✗ No shift — pressure doesn't affect gaseous equilibria.  

**L13.T1.A2.q09** · ERR_CATALYST_SHIFT  
Q: Does adding a CATALYST shift the position of an equilibrium?  
- ✗ Yes — a catalyst shifts the equilibrium toward the products.  
- ✗ Yes — a catalyst increases the value of Kc.  
- ✅ No — a catalyst speeds both directions equally; equilibrium is reached faster at the same position.  

**L13.T1.A2.q10** · ERR_HABER_CONDITIONS  
Q: Describe the conditions used in the HABER process for producing ammonia (N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, exothermic).  
- ✅ Moderate temperature (~450 °C), high pressure (~200 atm), an iron catalyst.  
- ✗ Very low temperature (~25 °C) and low pressure (1 atm).  
- ✗ Very high temperature (~1000 °C) and a platinum catalyst.  

**L13.T1.A2.q11** · ERR_CONTACT_PROCESS  
Q: Describe the CONTACT process (sulfuric acid manufacture) in outline.  
- ✗ N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ over an iron catalyst.  
- ✅ S → SO₂; then 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃ (V₂O₅ catalyst, ~450 °C); then SO₃ → H₂SO₄.  
- ✗ Electrolysis of water to make sulfuric acid.

- ✅ Q rises above Kc, so the equilibrium shifts backward (to the left).
